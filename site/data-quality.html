Missing required identity or location

Shared coordinate groups

Review

21

Method and provenance

Method: Group records that have exactly equal numeric latitude and longitude values.

Known limitation: Shared coordinates are review prompts, not errors; multiple clubs can legitimately use one ground.

Likely duplicate groups

Method and provenance

Method: Group normalized club, pitch, county, and country names at coordinates rounded to five decimal places.

Source: canonical_dataset

Known limitation: Candidates require human review because a club can have multiple records at one complex.

Valid directions links

Pass

100.0%

Method and provenance

Method: Require an allowed Google Maps host, one finite daddr pair, and coordinates matching the canonical point within 0.00001 degrees.

Enrichment coverage

Completeness of optional context fields and the separately dated OpenStreetMap geometry snapshot.

Elevation coverage

Pass

99.8%

1,986 of 1,990 records include a valid elevation value.

Method and provenance

Method: Count nonblank finite elevation values between -500 and 9,000 metres.

Source: canonical_dataset

Data as of: 2026-09-19

Known limitation: Elevation values are enrichment estimates and are not surveyed ground heights.

Rainfall coverage

Pass

99.8%

1,986 of 1,990 records include both rainfall fields.

Method and provenance

Method: Count rows with valid annual rainfall and rain-days values within the published schema ranges.

Source: canonical_dataset

Data as of: 2026-09-19

Known limitation: Rainfall fields are modelled annual estimates rather than observations at each pitch.

Reviewed Wikipedia links

Information

56.6%

1,126 of 1,990 records include a reviewed Wikipedia link.

Method and provenance

Method: Count nonblank Wikipedia URLs that use an allowed Wikipedia hostname.

Source: canonical_dataset

Data as of: 2026-09-19

Known limitation: Not every club has a suitable Wikipedia article, so no completeness target is applied.

OSM geometry-match coverage

Information

96.3%

1,621 of 1,683 evaluated snapshot rows have a nearby OSM pitch match.

Method and provenance

Method: Treat matched_gaa and matched_generic as matches, no_match as evaluated nonmatches, and exclude unavailable statuses from the denominator.

Source: osm_coverage_snapshot

Data as of: 2026-07-05

Known limitation: This is a historical proximity check against OSM pitch geometry, not proof that every match is correctly tagged. The snapshot contains 1,683 rows while the current canonical dataset contains 1,687 Ireland records.
